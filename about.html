About.

I’m Carlos Ezequiel Tovar Jovel, a student from La Libertad, El Salvador. I love turning ideas into things people can use: websites, small electronics builds and school projects that bring a whole team together.

My main strengths are leading teams, learning fast and staying consistent. I went from struggling with English to winning a scholarship to Washington D.C. with the Youth Ambassadors program. I also volunteer with Glasswing and EcoGuardians, and I’m working toward a career in chemical engineering.
